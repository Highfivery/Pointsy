Parents can cash in a reward for a kid on the spot (#189). On a kid's award
screen, Deduct now shows the family's rewards: tap one, confirm, and the points
come off right away, recorded as that reward. "I'm handing it over now" is
ticked by default; untick it to send the reward to Awaiting delivery instead.
The usual rules apply, so a reward the kid can't afford stays locked and says
how many more points they need. If the kid already asked for that reward, the
request is approved instead of charging twice. Paid-for rewards can now be
refunded from the activity list, which gives the points back without touching
milestone progress. Approving a reward is also safe when two parents act at
the same moment, and kids can no longer request another kid's personal reward.
